Artist & Designer Masha Archer
The Story & Heritage

A Legacy of Sculptural Royalty

"A creator of wearable art that celebrates history, exquisite craftsmanship, and majestic grandeur."

Born in Kiev, Ukraine, to renowned artists and art institute professors Sophia and Mykola Muchin, Masha emigrated to the United States in 1949 and has resided in San Francisco since 1967. Having studied painting and graphic design at the prestigious Pratt Institute in New York City, Masha eventually channeled her artistic genius into jewelry design—her most enduring and lauded vocation.

Recognized as 'Art-to-Wear Jewelry Designer, USA' by the New York Fashion Group, Masha's creations are treasured globally. Her pieces reside in museum collections, major television broadcasts (such as Sex and the City), and the personal collections of Hollywood elites like Oprah Winfrey and Beyoncé.

"My jewelry is not merely decorative; it is armor of artistic expression, designed to celebrate the strength and elegance of the wearer."
Aesthetic Philosophy

Maximalist Artistry & Grandeur

Each piece by Masha Archer is a balance of tribal authenticity, ancient history, and regal modern design.

One-of-a-Kind Originality

Every single piece is entirely handmade and unique. Masha never repeats a design, ensuring your statement piece remains a personal signature unmatched anywhere in the world.

Rare & Diverse Materials

Combining antique glass beads, semi-precious stones, lapis lazuli, coral, turquoise, amber, copal, chalcedony, and hand-cast metal alloys into multi-stranded compositions.

Architectural Sculptures

Her signature forms—collars, ziggurats, wreaths, and gorgets—blend Byzantine royalty and Ukrainian folk heritage into wearable sculptures of distinction.
